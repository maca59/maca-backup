# Privacy Policy — maca BackUp

**Document version 1.1**

This Privacy Policy describes how **maca BackUp** handles data when the plugin is used on your WordPress site. The product name is maca BackUp (not “Pro”). Provider: Maca Development ([maca.se](https://maca.se/)).

## 1. Backup data

Backups may include files and database content from your site (themes, plugins, uploads, posts, users, settings, and similar). That data stays under **your control**: on your server and/or in cloud accounts you configure. maca does not receive or host backup archives.

## 2. Credentials you enter

Storage credentials (FTP passwords, API tokens, S3 keys, OAuth tokens, and similar) are stored in your WordPress database or options as configured by the plugin. They are used only to transfer backups to destinations you choose. Keep your WordPress admin and hosting secure. maca does not collect these credentials for hosting backups.

## 3. Optional telemetry / hub

Hub/telemetry is **off by default**. If you enable it under Settings → maca Hub, limited metadata may be sent to `api.maca.se`—for example the site URL, plugin version, and backup history entries (type, date/time, size, status). Backup file contents and database dumps are not uploaded to maca. You can turn this off again at any time in Settings.

## 4. Support form and contact

If you use the in-plugin support form on the Support tab, the plugin may send your name, email address, subject, message, site URL, plugin version, and optional system information to maca.se, where tickets are handled via Fluent Support (or equivalent). If you email [support@maca.se](mailto:support@maca.se), we process the information you provide (and any diagnostics you choose to share) to respond to your request.

## 5. Your obligations

You are the controller of personal data contained in your site backups. You are responsible for retention settings, restore testing, destination security, and ensuring you have a lawful basis to process and store that data.

## 6. Third parties

Remote storage providers you connect (for example Google, Dropbox, Microsoft, FTP hosts, or S3-compatible services) process data under their own terms. maca is not responsible for those services. Support tooling on maca.se (including Fluent) processes support submissions as described above.
